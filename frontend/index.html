CORE CAPABILITIES

Built for Modern Family Clinics

100% Offline Capability

Operates flawlessly without active internet connectivity. All family records, billing, and prescription data remain instantly accessible.

Family ID Grouping

Links household members under a single Family Head ID, preventing duplicate registrations and providing a full family health ledger.

Standardized Prescriptions

Generates clear, patient-friendly digital prescriptions with precise dosage, frequency, and attached disease-wise dietary charts.

Instant Record Search

Search patient records by name, family head, or mobile number within seconds. Displays active allergy profiles and pending balances.

Payment & Dues Tracking

Tracks consultation charges, partial payments, and outstanding balances with automated payment receipt generation.

Area-Wise Reports

Analyzes clinic growth by area (Navrangpura, Vastrapur, Satellite, Thaltej, Bopal), doctor-wise consultations, and daily revenue.
